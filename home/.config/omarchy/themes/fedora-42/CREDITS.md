Fedora 42 theme for Omarchy
===========================

An Adwaita dark theme after Fedora 42 Workstation (GNOME 48): Adwaita
surfaces (#242424 / #303030), the Adwaita accent blue (#3584e4), and the
libadwaita palette for terminals and editors.

Wallpapers
----------
backgrounds/0-f42-night.jpg and backgrounds/1-f42-day.jpg are the Fedora 42
default night and day wallpapers, from the f42-backgrounds-base package
(42.0.0-3.fc42).

  Files: f42-01-night.jxl, f42-01-day.jxl
  Authors: Fedora Design Team
  License: CC-BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

They are unmodified apart from conversion from JPEG XL to JPEG.
